## Audio and external music services

The ambient sound is an original oscillator-based soundscape synthesized in the
browser. It contains no samples or melodies from the featured songs or games.
The public edition does not fetch or play iTunes preview recordings, display
Apple album artwork, or cache commercial audio. Song buttons open an external
Apple Music search page; listening is handled by that service under its terms.
The project neither sells nor redistributes the featured music.

## Text and privacy

Short song notes are editorial interpretations, not artist quotations or
verified accounts of the writing sessions. The selection is curated and is not
a claim of an objective top-five ranking. Artist and track names identify the
subjects of the commentary. No lyric excerpts are intentionally included.

The app has no account system, analytics, backend database or API keys. Language
preference is stored in browser localStorage when available. Optional photos
make requests to Wikimedia; the stylesheet loads Barlow Condensed and Inter
through Google Fonts (not bundled in the repository); external music links make requests only when
opened. Those providers receive ordinary network information such as IP
addresses. The hosting provider may keep its own access logs.

## Corrections

Please open a GitHub issue with the affected artist, image URL or text and the
requested correction. If rights or metadata cannot be established, remove the
photo or use the built-in poster.
